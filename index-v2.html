About Us

The Heritage of The Royal Shepherd Worldwide Nigeria

Established on May 10, 2002, The Royal Shepherd Worldwide Nigeria stands as a proud department of CAC Agbala Itura, the Vineyard of Comfort founded by Prophet S.K. Abiara. The department was birthed to shape disciplined youths who serve God and humanity with excellence.

Our mission extends beyond ceremonial roles. We build character through responsibility, self-control, and teamwork while serving the church, the community, and the nation with humility.

Memorize and Practice

The Ten Laws of Royal Shepherd

  1. Love God with all your heart and above all else.
  2. Love your fellow human beings as yourself and be always kind and helpful.
  3. Accept Jesus Christ as your Lord and Saviour, confess Him and remain firmly loyal to Him for ever.
  4. Study the Holy Bible daily and be always guided by the word of God.
  5. Be prayerful at all times and pray for others always.
  6. Always lead a holy life; be clean in thought, word and deed.
  7. Attend church services regularly; participate actively and be useful.
  8. Be loyal and obedient to the Royal Shepherds officers and its policies and regulations.
  9. Receive, possess and maintain the Holy Spirit of God and always be controlled by Him.
  10. Preach Christ’s Gospel everyday and be ever thirsty for opportunities to save souls.

Values & Pledge

The Symbolism of Our Standard

Every color on our flag speaks to the noble standards that guide the Royal Shepherds.

Royal Blue

Represents loyalty, commitment, and unwavering faithfulness to the call of Christ.

Brilliant Gold

Reflects the worth and values that every Royal Shepherd must uphold before God and people.

Purity White

Symbolizes holiness, innocence, and a life that stands pure in the eyes of the Lord.

Royal Shepherd Worldwide Nigeria Pledge

“On my honour I pledge to be absolutely faithful and loyal to God Almighty and to Christ Apostolic Church, to further the cause of Christianity, always to help people in need, and to obey the laws of Royal Shepherd. So help me God.”
